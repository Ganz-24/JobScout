Unlock LinkedIn, Indeed & more

LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor block direct scraping. You can still include their listings through free-tier aggregator APIs. Copy .env.example to .env, add your keys, then restart the server.

  • JSearch (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter) — RAPIDAPI_KEY
  • Adzuna — ADZUNA_APP_ID, ADZUNA_APP_KEY, optional ADZUNA_COUNTRY
  • USAJOBS — USAJOBS_KEY, USAJOBS_EMAIL
